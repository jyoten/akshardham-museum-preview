CHAPTER 01

The original

Dholavira is one of the largest known cities of the Harappan (Indus–Sarasvati) civilisation, on an island in the Rann of Kutch in Gujarat.

Its builders laid out a fortified citadel and a town, and managed scarce water with an elaborate system of channels and reservoirs. A large inscription in the Harappan script was found near one of its gateways.

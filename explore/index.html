Four ways into India

Four galleries across three floors: monuments in stone, lifelike scenes, the ideas of scripture, and India’s living culture.

01

Lifelike Scenes & Statues

Walk among life-sized rishis, acharyas and leaders in full-scale scenes of India’s past.
02

Legacy Architecture

Scaled replicas of Ajanta and Ellora, grand mandir shikhars and more.
03

Walking into Scripture

Yoga, karma, ahimsa and bhakti, and the shared roots of India’s dharmic traditions.
04

A Living Tapestry

Festivals, food, clothing and dance — with interactive stations for young visitors.

Featured replica

The Ajanta Caves

Carved by hand into a horseshoe-shaped cliff in Maharashtra over some seven centuries, the original caves hold some of the finest surviving painting and sculpture of ancient India. Step inside a faithful replica of one of its great prayer halls.

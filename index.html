Telling our story, our way

If we do not tell our story, others will tell it for us.

The Akshardham Museum is a one-of-a-kind cultural immersion. Across three floors, lifelike scenes, scaled replicas of India’s great monuments and immersive shows trace a civilization from its first cities to the present day.

For our children

A confident answer to “Who am I?” — pride, understanding and belonging, discovered one gallery at a time.

For the world

An authentic gateway to India’s contributions to science, ethics, art and spirituality — beyond brief impressions.

Featured replica

The Ajanta Caves

Carved by hand into a horseshoe-shaped cliff in Maharashtra over some seven centuries, the original caves hold some of the finest surviving painting and sculpture of ancient India. Step inside a faithful replica of one of its great prayer halls.
